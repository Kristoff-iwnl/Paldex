Sources and coordinate convention
---------------------------------
- https://paldb.cc/js/map_data_en.js was downloaded and verified. Its regionData entries provide the in-game X/Y (ipos) values used for the 66 directly labelled regions.
- Sealed Realm of Terraria uses the explicit (-422, -795) alpha coordinate recorded in pal_locations.json; it is marked medium confidence because it is an alpha representative point rather than a regionData label.
- PalDB's dungeon-marker records use a different world-position format and do not provide a stated conversion to regionData ipos. Those names remain unknown to avoid fabricating or mixing coordinate systems.
